## Reliability findings

- **MFS doesn't enforce chmod.** Anything that needs strict perms (private keys especially) needs to live on the container's local FS (`/root/.ssh/`) or be copied there on each boot. The persistent source can stay on /workspace.
- **Don't assume a "global key" works on fresh remote machines.** Passphrase protection, key-type mismatches, and storage-location ambiguity all bit us. Generating fresh per-pod is faster and cleaner.
- **Per-pod SSH key is now part of the pattern's deploy steps.** Should be added to the playbook.
